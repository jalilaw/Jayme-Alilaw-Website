My story

I learned authority in rooms that didn't give it easily.

I'm a classically trained opera singer with advanced degrees in Vocal Performance. I've spent more than 21 years on professional stages, including a decade as a Resident Artist with The Atlanta Opera. I originated the role of The Mother in I Can't Breathe, an opera by Brandon J. Gibson and Leslie Burrs, and I champion the work of African American composers.

I'm also an Army veteran. I sang at military ceremonies around the world, including during my deployment to Northern Iraq.

I've taught since 2006. I founded and led Music After School from 2011 to 2018, serving more than 150 students a year across metro Atlanta. From 2012 to 2021 I was Lead Coach and Co-Producer of the Otis Redding Music Camp, where young songwriters wrote, recorded and performed their own music.

Coaching grew out of all of it. I'm an ICF Member Coach, and I've coached leaders and teams at Kaiser Permanente, Wells Fargo, Lincoln Center, the Doris Duke Foundation, Sandy Hook Promise and Kennesaw State. My company is called From The Core Coaching because the core is where the voice comes from. For a singer that's literal: real sound starts with breath deep in the body, not in the throat. The core is also the self under the roles, the armor and the performance, and that's what I help leaders speak from. And it's the soul: who you were before the world told you who to be. Authority works like a voice. It resonates out from the center.

Spiritualist

Why I say spiritualist

A spiritualist is someone who views spirit as a primary element of reality. That's how I work. I read birth charts the same way I coach: to give you language for what's already true about you.
