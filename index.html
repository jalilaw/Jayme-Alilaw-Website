About

One voice, many registers.

I've spent more than 21 years on professional stages, including a decade as a Resident Artist with The Atlanta Opera. I served in the Army. I coach leaders one on one, and I read charts. If you are wondering how that fits together, it's the same work every time: finding the true sound of a person and giving it room to flow.

Opera taught me that presence lives in the body. The Army taught me that authority is more than rank and title. Coaching taught me that most people already know what they need to say. They need a place to say it out loud first.
