Who it's for

For people who followed the script well and are quietly done with it.

  • You just got the promotion, or just lost the thing, and neither one feels like yours.
  • You've always felt different, and you're tired of explaining it.
  • People keep telling you what you're good at. You want to know how you're built.
  • You're at a crossroads, a new role, a birthday that lands hard, a Saturn return, and you want to choose the next step on purpose.
  • You've lost the ability to dream, or your vision has gotten murky, and you want to remember what lights you up.
The authority it builds

Inner authority.

Most of us learned to look outside ourselves for permission: the boss, the title, the five-year plan. Inner authority is knowing how you make good decisions, and trusting that enough to act on it.

This is how I help leaders turn their authenticity into authority, starting from the inside.

I use astrology and tarot as reflective tools. They don't predict your future. They give us a shared language for your patterns, your timing and your design, and coaching turns that language into a choice.

What happens

Five movements, one clear direction.

  1. Your question

    We start with coaching: what you need clarity on, and what sits underneath it.

  2. Your chart as a map

    I walk you through your chart in plain language, as a map of your patterns, timing and design.

  3. What comes up

    Something usually lands in the middle of the session. We stop and coach it right there.

  4. One card

    A tarot pull to check the direction we've found.

  5. Your next move

    We close with clear next steps. One client called them her marching orders.

“In two hours, you gave me clear marching orders on how to get things done in my life.”
Gwendolyn Kuhlmann · Soul Chart Session client

What it isn't

It isn't a psychic reading, and it isn't therapy. I'm an ICF Member Coach, and I keep this work inside a coaching container. You bring the question. You make the call.
