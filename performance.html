On stage

Jayme Alilaw

California-born and Atlanta-based, Jayme Alilaw sings across the operatic, classical, and spiritual repertoire. As a Resident Artist with The Atlanta Opera for more than a decade, she sang roles including Susanna in Le Nozze di Figaro and Maria in En Mis Palabras.

She champions the music of African American composers, from Florence Price, William Grant Still, and Margaret Bonds to Rosephanye Powell and B.E. Boykin. That commitment shapes every program she builds.

An Army veteran with advanced degrees in Vocal Performance, Jayme has performed in opera, concert, and theater across the American South. She is co-founder of Good Soil Production + Management, which produces outdoor concerts, short operas, and multidisciplinary work, including the Good Soil: Garden Gallery Series.

The work

I

Black Lilith Burlesque

One-Woman Show · Recital · Theater

An operatic excavation of exile, sovereignty, and shadow. Drawing from Florence Price, Puccini, Rosephanye Powell, William Grant Still, and B.E. Boykin, this work maps the Lilith archetype onto the lived experience of the Black woman: cast out, yet sovereign. Developed through the Reiser Lab Grant and artist residency retreats.

II

Sang Ms. Jayme

Theatrical Autobiography · One-Woman Show

A theatrical autobiography told through opera, Spirituals, R&B, and rap. From the Army to The Atlanta Opera to spiritual awakening, this is the full arc, sung and spoken. A work about what happens when a woman stops explaining herself and simply becomes who she already was.

III

La Femme Noire: The Celebrated Woman

Concert Program · Educational Residency

A multidisciplinary concert celebrating the work of African American women composers, poets, and singers across time. An act of preservation and proclamation, making audible what has been systemically silenced. Available as a concert program or multi-day educational residency.
