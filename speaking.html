The talks

Three talks, one framework

Signature keynote · also a workshop

Authenticity into Authority

Most leaders are taught to sound like someone else to be taken seriously. This talk shows the room another way: authority that comes from knowing who you are, standing on it and saying it out loud. It moves through the three pillars of The Resonant Voice, with stories from the opera stage, the Army and the coaching room.

People leave withLanguage for their own values, and one conversation they're ready to have.

Keynote or workshop

The Promotion Gap

The title arrives before the authority does. This talk is for newly promoted leaders, and the ones doing the job without the title yet. We name the gap between the seat and the voice, and close it: how you speak, decide and hold a room once the job is yours.

People leave withA clear picture of where they're still asking permission, and what to do instead.

Workshop or half-day for teams

Courageous Leadership

For people with influence who care about justice and feel frozen by the size of it. Shame keeps you still. Courage moves you. This session gives teams a way to act that feels honest, without performing or getting lost in guilt.

People leave withOne honest next step they can take inside their own role.
