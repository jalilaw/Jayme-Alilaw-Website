Who it's for

For leaders who got the title and are still waiting to feel it.

  • You were promoted to director or above, and the room still treats you like the person you used to be.
  • You rehearse every sentence before the meeting and replay it after.
  • You've been doing the bigger job for a year without the title, and you're tired of waiting to be noticed.
  • You're the only one at the table who looks or sounds like you, and you've been editing yourself to fit.
  • You want your pay, your title and your relationships at work to match what you can actually do.
The authority it builds

Positional authority.

Positional authority comes with the seat: the title, the budget, the decisions with your name on them. Most new leaders get the title before they feel the authority. They keep waiting for permission that already came with the job.

Coaching closes that gap. We work on how you speak, decide and hold a room, until the authority on the org chart and the authority people feel from you are the same thing.

The work rests on my framework, The Resonant Voice: an authentic, embodied and empowered voice.

What happens

How we work

  1. A chemistry call

    A free 20-minute call to see if we fit. You tell me what's going on, and I tell you honestly whether I'm the right coach for it.

  2. Weekly sessions

    Sixty minutes, one on one. We start with what's live that week: the meeting, the conversation, the decision you keep putting off.

  3. New awareness

    We get clear on what's in the way of where you want to go. Most of it is hard to see from the inside. Once you can name it, you can move past it.

  4. A plan that's yours

    Together we build a plan around your goals, your life, and the way you actually work.

  5. Between sessions

    The work happens outside of session. When something comes up midweek, you can reach me.
