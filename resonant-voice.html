The Resonant Voice™ · My framework

A voice that is seen, heard and felt.

The Resonant Voice is the framework under everything I do: how I coach, read a chart, speak, write and perform. Three pillars: Know it. Stand on it. Declare it.

See the three pillars Read The Resonant Voice
What resonance is

Resonance is more than volume.

It is the undeniable presence that comes from being deeply connected to one's truth, and amplifying that truth in a way that naturally draws others in. It carries strength, clarity, and lasting influence. A resonant voice is not just heard. It creates vibrations that shift spaces, impacts those it encounters, and is sustained with intention and authenticity.

Where it came from

It started in the practice room.

I built this over more than two decades as an opera singer, vocal coach and leadership coach. For years, these parts of me felt disconnected, like mismatched pieces of a puzzle. It wasn't until I learned to ground myself in my values and lead from my truth that the picture came into focus.

The voice is still where I learn. What I find in the practice room and the voice studio shows up later in a coaching session, a chart reading or an essay.

I don't offer my path as a model to follow. I live as specifically as I can as myself, so the work can move you toward your own equally specific truth.

The three pillars

Know it. Stand on it. Declare it.

I

The Authentic Voice

Know it · Who I Am

What are you made of?

Authenticity is not performance, it is recognition. The authentic voice grows according to its own range and capacity, anchored in values that don't shift with moods or seasons. This pillar is about uncovering what you are made of, so that everything you create, say, and lead flows from that place.

The workNaming your core values as operational anchors, not aspirational labels. Values that stay constant when feelings shift.

I am who I am at my core. I grow according to my own capacity. Know it.

II

The Embodied Voice

Stand on it · The Shape of Me

Where do you begin and end?

Embodiment is the practice of being in tune with body, mind, and creation. It is fortified by healthy boundaries, not walls, but the structures that maintain the integrity of your being. This pillar is about knowing where you begin and end, so that your presence has shape and your work has form.

The workBoundaries as structure. When we are clear on who we are and what we are about, we are free to choose and to not choose.

In tune with body, mind, and creation. Fortified by boundaries. Stand on it.

III

The Empowered Voice

Declare it · Self Authority as Leadership

Where does your authority come from?

Empowerment is internally sourced and outwardly expressed. It is the capacity to lead from within, to declare your truth, set direction, and inspire others not from a title or permission granted externally, but from deep recognition of your own authority.

The workLocating your authority inside yourself, not in a title or someone's permission. Then using it: declaring what's true for you, setting direction and leading others from there.

Internally powered. Externally expressed. Declare it.
