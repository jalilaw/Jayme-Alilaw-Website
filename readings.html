How I read

Spirit first. Then the chart.

I'm a spiritualist: I see spirit as a primary element of reality, and astrology is one of the languages I use to read it. I'm also an ICF Member Coach, so a reading with me stays practical.

A chart doesn't predict your future. It gives us shared language for your patterns, your timing and your design.

You bring the question. I bring the chart, and we talk it through until it means something you can use.

Before you book

Have these ready.

  • Birth dateDay, month and year.
  • Birth timeAs exact as you can find it. A birth certificate is the best source.
  • Birth placeThe city or town you were born in.

If you don't know your birth time, email me before you book and we'll talk about what's possible.

What a reading isn't

It isn't a psychic reading, and it isn't therapy. I won't tell you what's going to happen. I'll help you see what's there, and you make the call.
